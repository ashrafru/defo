How to bring the charts from your computer

On the computer, open the workbench, then Central Scrutinizer › Backup. It downloads a file named like CentralScrutinizer_Database_2026-10-29.awbdb. Copy that file to this phone (email, Drive, WhatsApp or USB), then tap Import backup here and choose it. Your charts are kept on this phone and appear under My charts. Export my charts writes the same kind of file, which Central Scrutinizer › Restore on the computer can read.
