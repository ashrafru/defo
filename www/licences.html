Licences and source

The mobile edition is a phone and tablet version of the Polaris Launcher controller. Its source is the plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript of this app (index.html, app.css, app.js, loader.js, sw.js), released under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later, like the desktop controller.

The 36 original HTML tools are stored in compressed parts and rebuilt on your device byte for byte. Each rebuilt tool is checked against its SHA-256 checksum before it opens. Their code, copyrights, engine source archives and legal notices are unchanged. Consult each tool for its notices.
